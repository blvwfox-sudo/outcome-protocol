Super Soldier Protocol™

Goal: Performance + fat loss + muscle + recovery. Duration: 6–12 weeks. Level: Intermediate–Advanced. This protocol is a tactical framework – your clinician controls the actual dosing, timing, and therapy decisions.

Peptide Stack & Roles

  • CJC/IPA – Growth hormone–supporting combo that may enhance recovery, sleep quality, fat loss, and lean mass under medical supervision.
  • BPC-157 – Recovery-focused peptide investigated for roles in soft-tissue and gut support in research settings.
  • TB-500 – Systemic repair peptide explored for mobility, tissue healing, and inflammation modulation.
  • GHK-Cu – Copper-binding peptide linked to collagen remodeling, tissue quality, and cosmetic/longevity applications.
  • AOD-9604 – GH fragment studied for fat-loss support without the full hormonal profile of growth hormone.
  • IGF-1 (optional) – Advanced growth factor sometimes layered into performance blocks under close clinician oversight.

Who This Is For

  • Wants a structured, goal-first framework instead of random add-ons.
  • Is willing to track sleep, training, nutrition, and recovery markers.
  • Is working with, or actively seeking, a qualified medical professional.
  • Understands this is education only – not a prescription.

Educational Dosing & Timing Example

This example shows how reconstitution and syringe units can be calculated. It is not telling you what to take. Always confirm real numbers with your clinician and pharmacy.

  • Example vial: 5 mg peptide reconstituted with 2.5 mL bacteriostatic water.
  • Concentration: 5 ÷ 2.5 = 2.0 mg/mL = 2000 mcg/mL.
  • Target educational dose: 250 mcg.
  • Volume per example dose: 250 ÷ 2000 = 0.125 mL.
  • On a 100-unit insulin syringe (1 mL = 100 units), 0.125 mL ≈ 12.5 units.

Use the Outcome Planner to plug in actual vial amounts, diluent volume, and target doses from your clinician.

Deployment Notes

  • Run in clearly defined blocks (6–12 weeks) with a planned start and end.
  • Align your training and nutrition to the goal of this protocol.
  • Schedule regular check-ins for labs, side-effects, and progress.
  • Track simple metrics weekly: sleep, energy, mood, performance, and body comp.
Is this telling me exactly what to take?

No. Outcome is an educational and strategic framework only. All dosing, timing, and medication decisions belong to your clinician.

Can I modify the stack with my clinician?

Yes – the real power here is having a shared language. You can remove, swap, or re-order components with your clinician while staying aligned with the mission.
