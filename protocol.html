Outcome Protocol Playbook

A zoomed‑in look at how to phase, stack, and evaluate your plan with your medical team.

Wolverine Track · Tissue & Joint Repair

Objective: restore function, reduce downtime, and protect future training blocks.

  • Anchor around BPC‑157 and TB‑500, as appropriate and medically supervised.
  • Emphasize protein intake, collagen, vitamin C, and sleep opportunity.
  • Conservative loading progression: range of motion → stability → load.
  • Weekly Outcome metrics: pain, ROM, confidence under load, sleep quality.

Recomp Track · Body Composition & Metabolic Health

Objective: reduce fat mass, protect or increase lean tissue, and improve labs.

  • Potential integration of GLP‑1/GIP agonists (e.g., Tirzepatide) or other tools under medical care.
  • Structured resistance training 3–4x/week plus daily walking target.
  • Protein‑forward nutrition with appropriate calorie range and fiber.
  • Weekly metrics: scale weight, waist, progress photos, strength anchors.

Operator Track · Performance & Cognitive Resilience

Objective: blend stamina, strength, clarity, and emotional bandwidth.

  • Support GH axis where appropriate: Sermorelin/Ipamorelin/Tesamorelin via MD.
  • Intentional sleep, circadian, and light management protocols.
  • Conditioning built around your real‑world stress and work schedule.
  • Metrics: strength, conditioning benchmarks, subjective focus and mood.

How to Use this Playbook

  1. Print or export this page as a PDF.
  2. Highlight anything that seems relevant to your situation.
  3. Review with your clinician and adjust based on their expertise.
  4. Use the Outcome scorecard weekly to track response and side‑effects.

Reminder: this is an educational structure only. No specific dosing or therapy is being recommended.
