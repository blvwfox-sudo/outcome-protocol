Peptide Library (Outcome Edition)

A high-level educational overview of the compounds used across the Outcome protocols. This is a primer, not a replacement for primary literature or prescribing information.

BPC-157

Recovery · Tissue Support

Frequently discussed in research contexts around gut integrity and soft-tissue repair (tendons, ligaments, and muscle).

TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4 fragment)

Systemic Repair

Explored in animal and early human settings for systemic healing, cell migration, and mobility following injury.

GHK-Cu

Collagen · Skin · Hair

Popular in cosmetic and longevity spaces via its links to collagen remodeling, skin quality, and hair support.

CJC/IPA

GH Axis Support

Combination of a GHRH analog and a secretagogue used to stimulate endogenous GH under medical supervision.

AOD-9604

Fat Loss

Fragment of the growth hormone molecule evaluated for fat-loss support without the full metabolic profile of GH.

IGF-1

Muscle · Performance

Potent growth factor sometimes used in advanced performance contexts; requires tight monitoring and expert oversight.
