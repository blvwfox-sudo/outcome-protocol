Peptide Reference · Outcome Edition

A high‑level educational overview of the compounds referenced throughout the Outcome framework. This is not exhaustive and does not replace primary literature or medical guidance.

BPC‑157

Tissue & GI Support · Research Compound

BPC‑157 is a synthetic peptide fragment derived from a naturally occurring gastric protein. In animal and preclinical settings it has been explored for gut integrity, tendon and ligament repair, and systemic recovery support.

Humans studies are limited. Use in humans is considered experimental and should only be discussed in the context of legitimate medical research or clinical oversight where allowed.

TB‑500 (Thymosin Beta‑4 fragment)

Systemic Tissue Support · Research Compound

TB‑500 is a synthetic fragment of the Thymosin Beta‑4 protein. In animal models it has been evaluated for recovery from musculoskeletal and cardiovascular injuries, potentially via actin binding and cell migration pathways.

GHK‑Cu

Skin, Hair, and Tissue Health

GHK‑Cu is a naturally occurring copper‑binding peptide with a long history in cosmetic and topical applications. Literature has explored its roles in wound healing, collagen remodeling, and hair growth support.

Tirzepatide

Dual GIP/GLP‑1 RA · Prescription Only

Tirzepatide is an FDA‑approved injectable for type 2 diabetes with strong data in weight reduction. It acts as a dual agonist at GIP and GLP‑1 receptors, improving insulin sensitivity, appetite regulation, and glycemic control.

Retatrutide*

Triple Agonist · Investigational

Retatrutide is a triple agonist (GIP/GLP‑1/glucagon) being researched for aggressive weight reduction and metabolic improvements. Access is typically limited to clinical trial settings.

*Mentioned as an example of future‑leaning tools. It is not generally available for routine clinical use.

MOTS‑C

Mitochondrial‑Derived Peptide · Research

MOTS‑C is encoded in mitochondrial DNA and has been evaluated in early research for potential roles in exercise performance, insulin sensitivity, and metabolic resilience.

Sermorelin

GHRH Analog

Sermorelin is a growth hormone–releasing hormone analog that stimulates endogenous GH secretion in a physiologic, pulsatile manner when used clinically.

Ipamorelin

Ghrelin Mimetic · GHRP

Ipamorelin is a selective growth hormone secretagogue often paired with GHRH analogs to enhance GH release.

Tesamorelin

GHRH Analog · Visceral Fat Focus

Tesamorelin is an FDA‑approved GHRH analog used for the reduction of visceral adipose tissue in specific patient populations, with ongoing research in other areas.

How to Go Deeper

For each compound, search primary literature on databases such as PubMed and review official prescribing information where applicable. Use the Outcome framework to connect what you read with your individual goals and discuss options with your clinician.

This page intentionally avoids dosing numbers. Those decisions must always be individualized and made by a licensed medical professional.
