The Outcome Protocol Framework

One system, three phases, built around your recovery, composition, and performance objectives.

Phase I · Baseline & Repair

Weeks 1–4

  • Establish sleep, hydration, and step-count baselines.
  • Deploy foundational tissue repair stack (BPC‑157 + TB‑500).
  • Introduce low-dose GHK‑Cu for tissue and skin support.
  • Dial in protein, electrolyte, and micronutrient minimums.

Phase II · Metabolic & Recomposition

Weeks 5–12

  • Layer in GLP‑1/GIP support (e.g., Tirzepatide) if clinically appropriate.
  • Consider MOTS‑C for mitochondrial and exercise capacity support.
  • Program strength training and NEAT around recovery capacity.
  • Monitor waist, weight, and performance markers weekly.

Phase III · Performance & Longevity

Weeks 12+

  • Support GH axis via Sermorelin/Ipamorelin/Tesamorelin where appropriate.
  • Rotate in higher-demand blocks (strength, conditioning, or hypertrophy).
  • Integrate heat, cold, and zone‑2 protocols as recovery allows.
  • Quarterly review: labs, body comp, and performance KPIs.

Peptide Library · Outcome Edition

A clean, tactical overview of the core compounds referenced in the Outcome Protocol.

Recovery & Tissue Support

BPC‑157
Investigational peptide often used in research around gut and soft‑tissue support.
TB‑500 (Thymosin Beta‑4 fragment)
Explored for potential benefits in tissue recovery and systemic repair pathways.
GHK‑Cu
Copper‑binding peptide commonly studied in skin, hair, and tissue regeneration contexts.

Metabolic & Body Composition

Tirzepatide
Dual GIP/GLP‑1 receptor agonist used clinically for glycemic control and weight management.
Retatrutide*
Triple‑agonist (GIP/GLP‑1/glucagon) under investigation for aggressive body composition shifts.
MOTS‑C
Mitochondrial‑derived peptide being researched for exercise capacity and metabolic flexibility.

Growth Hormone Axis

Sermorelin
GHRH analog that stimulates endogenous growth hormone release in a pulsatile pattern.
Ipamorelin
Ghrelin mimetic and GHRP often paired with GHRH analogs for synergistic GH release.
Tesamorelin
GHRH analog with specific indications and research focus around visceral adipose tissue.

Outcome FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about how to use this system.

Is Outcome giving me medical advice or telling me how to dose?

No. Outcome is an educational and organizational framework. It helps you think clearly about phases, priorities, and questions to bring to your licensed provider. It does not replace medical guidance.

Can I use Outcome without already being on TRT or peptides?

Yes. Many people start with the non‑pharmacologic side: training, nutrition, sleep, and environmental inputs. Outcome simply gives you a tactical way to layer in therapies later, if appropriate and prescribed.

Does Outcome include exact dosing and timing?

The downloadable blueprint includes example reconstitution math and educational timing frameworks to discuss with your clinician. Final decisions around dosing and timing always belong to your medical team.

What if my goals are injury recovery plus fat loss?

The system was designed to stack objectives without overwhelming recovery. You can prioritize “Wolverine” style tissue repair first, then roll into recomposition once your foundations are handled.
